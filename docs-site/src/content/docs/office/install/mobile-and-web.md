## Android

An app for phones and tablets, with the same four apps and the same files.

## iPhone and iPad

An app for both. It needs the platform maker's developer programme, so it comes after Android.

## In your browser

Open and edit documents from Nubo Email's drive in any browser on a computer or phone, signed in with your Nubo account. This also brings editing with several people at once, on documents stored on a Nubo server.

Today, documents in Nubo Email open in the editor that Nubo Email already has. The web version of Nubo Office replaces it when it is ready.
