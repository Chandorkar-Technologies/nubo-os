Nubo Office is open source. It is built on Collabora Online and LibreOffice technology, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0).

## What that means for you

- You can read the source, and you can build it yourself.
- Our changes are published in the Nubo OS repository: https://github.com/Chandorkar-Technologies/nubo-os (the `office` folder).
- Licence texts are in the app: **Help**, then **About**, then **License Information**.
