Nubo Write is for anything made of paragraphs: letters, reports, essays, forms. It opens `.docx`, `.doc`, `.odt`, `.rtf` and `.txt` files.

## The window

- **Tabs:** File, Home, Insert, Layout, References, Review, Format, Form, View and Help.
- **Home** holds Paste and the clipboard tools, Font, Paragraph, a gallery of styles (Title, Subtitle, Heading, Body Text, Table Contents and more) and Insert.
- **Sidebar** on the right shows Style, Character and Paragraph settings for what you have selected.
- **Ruler** along the top and left of the page.
- **Status bar** shows the page count, word and character count, and the language.

## What you can do

- **Style a document.** Apply Title, Subtitle and Heading styles from the Home tab, and set fonts, sizes and spacing from the sidebar.
- **Tables.** Add a table from **Insert**, fill it and format its cells.
- **Lists and paragraph layout.** Bullets, numbering, indents, spacing and line spacing in the sidebar.
- **Review with other people.** The **Review** tab holds comments and tracked changes. <!-- verify label: Review tab entries -->
- **References.** The **References** tab covers tables of contents, footnotes and similar. <!-- verify label: References tab entries -->
- **Forms.** The **Form** tab adds form controls.
- **Search.** The Search tool on the Home tab finds text and replaces it.

## Verify

Open the sample: start **Nubo Write**, type a line, select it, and choose **Title** from the style gallery. The line takes the title style.
