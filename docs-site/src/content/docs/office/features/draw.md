Nubo Draw is for diagrams, flyers, posters and simple drawings. It opens `.odg` and `.vsd` files, and PDF files.

## The window

- **Tabs:** File, Home, Insert, Layout, Review, Format, View and Help.
- **Page** in the middle, on a dark workspace, with rulers.
- **Page list** on the left and **Page** settings (format, orientation, background, margin) in the sidebar.
- **Home** has Text Box, Illustrations (shapes), Insert and Search.

## What you can do

- **Shapes and text.** Draw boxes and ovals, put text inside them or in text boxes, and colour them.
- **Connectors.** Link two shapes with an arrow. It stays attached when you move either shape.
- **Several pages** in one drawing.
- **PDF.** Open a PDF to read or mark it up, and export a drawing as PDF. <!-- verify label: PDF export entry -->

## Verify

Open Nubo Draw and insert a shape from **Illustrations**. It appears on the page, and you can drag it.
