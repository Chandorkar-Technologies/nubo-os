Nubo Office is four apps in one window design. Each app has its own accent colour, matching its icon: **blue** for Write, **green** for Cells, **orange** for Present and **violet** for Draw.

## Tour of the apps

- [Nubo Write](/office/features/write/): documents, with styles, tables, references, review tools and forms.
- [Nubo Cells](/office/features/cells/): spreadsheets, with formulas, charts, pivot tables and data tools.
- [Nubo Present](/office/features/present/): presentations, with layouts, themes, transitions and a presenter view.
- [Nubo Draw](/office/features/draw/): diagrams, flyers and PDF.

## Across all four

- [Start screen and templates](/office/features/start-screen/): the first thing you see, and the File menu.
- [Files and formats](/office/features/formats/): what opens, what saves, and how well.
- [Themes and display](/office/features/themes-and-display/): dark and light, zoom, compact view and more.
- [Printing and export](/office/features/printing-and-export/): print, PDF, and saving a copy.
- [Accessibility](/office/features/accessibility/): the accessibility check and screen-reader support.
