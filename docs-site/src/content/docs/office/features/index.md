- [Files and formats](/office/features/formats/): every format Nubo Office opens and saves.
- [Start screen and templates](/office/features/start-screen/): the first thing you see.
- [Nubo Write](/office/features/write/): documents.
- [Nubo Cells](/office/features/cells/): spreadsheets.
- [Nubo Present](/office/features/present/): presentations.
- [Nubo Draw](/office/features/draw/): drawings and PDF.

All four apps share one window design: a menu of tabs along the top, a sidebar for formatting on the right, and a status bar along the bottom. Each app has its own accent colour, matching its icon: blue for Write, green for Cells, orange for Present and violet for Draw.
