Nubo Present is for talks and slide decks. It opens `.pptx`, `.ppt` and `.odp` files.

## The window

- **Tabs:** File, Home, Insert, Design, Transition, Slide Show, Review, Format, View and Help.
- **Navigation pane** on the left with the **Slides** list and an **Outline** view.
- **Slide** in the middle, with placeholders such as "Click to add Title".
- **Sidebar** with Slide settings (format, orientation, background, master slide) and a gallery of **Layouts**.
- **Buttons under the slide list** to add, duplicate and delete slides.

## What you can do

- **Pick a layout** for each slide from the gallery in the sidebar.
- **Add shapes, connectors, tables and charts** from the Home and Insert tabs.
- **Transitions.** Choose how one slide gives way to the next in the **Transition** tab.
- **Slide Show.** Start the show from the **Slide Show** tab. <!-- verify label: start from first and from current slide -->
- **Design.** The **Design** tab changes the look of the whole deck.
- **Slide format.** Widescreen 16:9 is the default.

## Verify

Add a slide with **New** on the Home tab. The slide list shows two slides, and the status bar reads "Slide 2 of 2".
