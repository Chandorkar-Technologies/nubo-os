## Is Nubo Office free?

Early access builds are free to download and use.

## Do I need a Nubo account?

No, not for files on your computer. You need one only to open documents stored on a Nubo server.

## Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first install, you can open, edit and save files on your computer offline. Documents on a server need a connection.

## Will my Word, Excel and PowerPoint files look right?

Most do. See [Files and formats](/office/features/formats/) for the formats and the limits.

## Which apps are in the suite?

Nubo Write for documents, Nubo Cells for spreadsheets, Nubo Present for presentations and Nubo Draw for drawings, plus the Nubo Office start screen.

## Which platforms does it run on?

Nubo OS and Linux first. Windows, macOS, Android, iPhone, iPad and the web are planned. See [Installing Nubo Office](/office/install/).

## Can several people edit one document at the same time?

Not yet. It is planned for documents stored on a Nubo server.

## Where do my files go?

Where you save them. Nubo Office does not move files anywhere on its own.
