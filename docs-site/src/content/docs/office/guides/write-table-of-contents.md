**Applies to:** Nubo Write

A table of contents is built from the headings in your document.

## Before you begin

The document uses **Heading** styles for its sections. Apply them from the style gallery on **Home**, or with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>5</kbd> for levels one to five.

## Steps

1. Click where the table should go, usually after the title.
2. Open the **References** tab.
3. Choose **Table of Contents and Index**. The table appears, with one line for each heading and its page number.
4. After you change the headings or the pages, choose **Update Index** or **Update All** in the same tab.

## Also in References

- **Footnote** and **Endnote**, with **Footnote/Endnote Settings**.
- **Bookmark** marks a place you can jump to.
- **Cross-reference** points to a heading, a table or a number elsewhere in the document.
- **Index Entry** marks a word for an index.

## Verify

Each heading appears in the table with the right page number. Change a heading, choose **Update All**, and the table follows.

## Troubleshooting

- **The table is empty.** No text uses a Heading style. Apply one and update.
- **Page numbers are wrong.** Choose **Update All** after the last edit.
