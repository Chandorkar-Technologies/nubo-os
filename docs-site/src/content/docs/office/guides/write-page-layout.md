**Applies to:** Nubo Write

## Before you begin

A document is open in Nubo Write.

## Set up the page

1. Open the **Layout** tab. The **Page Setup** group has **Margin**, **Size** and **Orientation**.
2. Choose **Margin** and pick one of the settings.
3. Choose **Size** for the paper, for example A4 or Letter.
4. Choose **Orientation** for portrait or landscape.
5. **Columns** splits the text into columns, for a newsletter.

## Breaks

- **Page Break** starts a new page. It is in **Layout** and in **Insert**.
- **Manual Break** in **Layout** offers other kinds of break, such as a column break.

## Header, footer and page numbers

1. Open the **Insert** tab. The **Header & Footer** group has **Header**, **Footer**, **Page Number** and **Field**.
2. Choose **Header**, and type the text that repeats at the top of every page.
3. Choose **Footer** for the bottom.
4. Choose **Page Number** to number the pages.

## Other layout tools

- **Hyphenation** and **Line Numbering** in Layout.
- **Watermark** puts faint text behind the page.
- **Title Page** (in Insert and Layout) makes a separate first page.
- **Wrap**, **Align** and **Arrange** control how pictures and shapes sit with the text.

## Verify

Scroll through the document. The header, footer and page numbers appear on every page.
