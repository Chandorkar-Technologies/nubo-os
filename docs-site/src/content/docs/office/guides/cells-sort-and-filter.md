**Applies to:** Nubo Cells

## Before you begin

A list with headings in the first row, with no empty rows in it.

## Sort

1. Click any cell in the list.
2. Open the **Data** tab.
3. Choose **Sort Ascending** or **Sort Descending** to sort by the column you clicked. **Sort** opens more choices, such as sorting by several columns.

## Filter

1. Click a cell in the list.
2. In **Data**, choose **AutoFilter**. Each heading gets a small arrow.
3. Click the arrow and choose the values to show.
4. **Reset Filter** shows all rows again. **Hide AutoFilter** removes the arrows.

For harder questions, **Standard Filter** lets you set conditions such as "greater than", and **Advanced Filter** reads its conditions from a range on the sheet.

## Group rows

**Group** in the Data tab gathers rows so you can fold them away. **Show Details** and **Hide Details** open and close a group. **Ungroup** and **Remove Outline** undo it.

## Statistics

**Statistics** in the Data tab gives summary figures for the selected cells.

## Verify

After a filter, the row numbers on the left skip the rows that are hidden.
