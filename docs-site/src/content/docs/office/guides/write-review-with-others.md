**Applies to:** Nubo Write

## Before you begin

A document is open. Everything here is in the **Review** tab.

## Comments

1. Select the words you want to comment on.
2. Choose **Comment** in the Review tab, or in the Insert tab.
3. Type your note.

**Show Comments** shows or hides the comments in the margin. **Resolved Comments** shows the ones that are done.

## Tracked changes

1. Open the **Tracking** menu in the Review tab and turn tracking on.
2. Edit the document. Your changes are marked instead of applied. <!-- verify label: the Tracking menu entries -->
3. The author of the document can accept or reject each change from the same menu.

## Compare two versions

**Compare** in the Review tab opens a second file and marks the differences from the current one.

## Other checks in Review

- **Word Count** and **Thesaurus**.
- **Language** sets the language of the selected text for spelling.
- **Spelling Options** and **Auto Spell Check**.
- **Accessibility Check**. See [Accessibility](/office/features/accessibility/).

## Verify

With tracking on, change a word. The change is marked, and you can accept or reject it.
