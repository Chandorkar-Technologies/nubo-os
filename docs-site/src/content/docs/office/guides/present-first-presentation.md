**Applies to:** Nubo Present

## Before you begin

Nubo Office is installed.

## Steps

1. Open **Nubo Present**. A first slide opens with a title and a subtitle placeholder.
2. Click "Click to add Title" and type the title. Click the lower placeholder and type the subtitle.
3. Add a slide: choose **New** on the **Home** tab.
4. Pick how the new slide is arranged from **Layouts** in the sidebar. For example, a title and one content box.
5. Add a shape: choose **Shapes** on the Home tab and drag on the slide. Type inside it to add text.
6. Add a chart or a table from the **Insert** tab.
7. Change the look of the whole deck: open **Design**, and choose a theme.
8. Move between slides in the list on the left, with **Slides**. **Outline** shows only the text.
9. Save with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>. Choose `.pptx` to share with PowerPoint users, or `.odp`.

## Verify

The slide list shows every slide, and the status bar reads "Slide 2 of 2".

## Troubleshooting

- **A placeholder does not show.** The layout of the slide has none. Choose another layout.
- **Text does not fit.** Make the box larger, or use less text.
