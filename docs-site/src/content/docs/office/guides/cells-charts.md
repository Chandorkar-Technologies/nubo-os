**Applies to:** Nubo Cells

## Before you begin

A sheet with a table, such as the budget from [Build your first spreadsheet](/office/guides/cells-first-spreadsheet/). The first row holds the headings.

## Steps

1. Select the range, including the headings, for example `A1:D7`.
2. Open the **Insert** tab, then **Illustrations**, and choose **Chart**. <!-- verify label: the chart entry in Illustrations -->
3. A chart appears on the sheet. Drag it to a free place so it does not cover your data.
4. To change it, select the chart and use the sidebar and the chart tools that appear.

The chart follows the data. Change a figure and the bars change.

## Move and resize

- Drag the chart to move it.
- Drag a corner to resize it.
- **Position and Size** in the **Format** tab sets exact values.

## Small charts in a cell

**Sparkline** in the **Format** tab draws a tiny chart inside a cell, to show a trend beside a row.

## Verify

The chart shows one group of bars for each month, and the legend matches the headings.

## Troubleshooting

- **The chart covers the data.** Drag it away, or set its position in Format.
- **Months are missing from the chart.** The range you selected did not include them. Select the chart's data again.
