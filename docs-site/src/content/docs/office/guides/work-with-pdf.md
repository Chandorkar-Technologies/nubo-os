## Open a PDF

Open a `.pdf` file with Nubo Draw. Each page of the PDF becomes a page of a drawing, and you can read it and add to it. <!-- verify: how complex PDF pages are edited -->

1. Right-click the PDF in Files and choose **Open With**, then **Nubo Draw**, or open it from the app with **File**, **Open**.
2. Move between pages in the list on the left.
3. Add text boxes, shapes and comments from the **Insert** tab.

## Make a PDF

From any app:

1. Open **File**, then **Export**.
2. Choose PDF and save.

## Troubleshooting

- **The text of a PDF looks broken.** PDF is a layout format, so pages with complex fonts can look different when edited. Use the PDF for reading, and edit the source document where you can.
