**Applies to:** Nubo Present

## Transitions

1. Select a slide in the list.
2. Open the **Transition** tab.
3. Choose a transition from the gallery: **None**, **Wipe**, **Wheel**, **Uncover** and others.
4. **Variant** changes the direction or style. **Duration** sets how long it takes.
5. Choose when it starts: **On mouse click**, or **After** a number of seconds.
6. **Apply to All Slides** uses the same transition for every slide.

## Start the show

Open the **Slide Show** tab:

- **From Beginning** starts at the first slide.
- **From Current Slide** starts at the slide you have selected.
- **Presenter View** shows the show to the audience and your notes and the next slide to you. It needs a second screen.
- **Present in Window** runs the show in a window and not full screen.

## Hide a slide

**Hide Slide** in the **Set Up** group keeps a slide in the deck but skips it in the show.

## During the show

Click or press <kbd>Space</kbd> or the right arrow to move on. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop. <!-- verify: the keys that move through the show -->

## Verify

Start **From Beginning**. The slides appear in order, with the transition between them.
