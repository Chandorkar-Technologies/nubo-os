**Applies to:** Nubo Write

In about ten minutes you will write a short report, style it, add a table and save a PDF.

## Before you begin

Nubo Office is installed. See [Start an app](/office/use/start-an-app/).

## Steps

1. Open **Nubo Write**. A blank document opens.
2. Type a title, such as `Quarterly report`, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. Click in the title line. On the **Home** tab, choose **Title** from the gallery of styles. The line becomes large and centred.
4. Type a subtitle on the next line, and choose **Subtitle** from the gallery.
5. Type `Highlights` on a new line. Choose **Heading** from the gallery, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd>.
6. Write a paragraph under it. The style is **Default Paragraph Style**: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> to apply it.
7. Add a table. Open the **Insert** tab, choose **Table**, and pick a size, for example three columns and four rows.
8. Type in the table. Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to move to the next cell.
9. Colour the header row: select the first row, then open **Format** and use the cell tools. <!-- verify label: the cell background control -->
10. Save: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> and choose a name and a folder. Keep `.docx` if you will share with Word users, or choose `.odt`.
11. Make a PDF copy: open **File**, then **Export**, and choose PDF.

## Verify

The saved file opens again with the same title, headings and table. The PDF shows the same page.

## Troubleshooting

- **A style does not change the text.** The cursor was not inside the paragraph. Click in the line and apply the style again.
- **Text jumps to the next page.** Check the margins in the **Layout** tab. See [Page layout, headers and footers](/office/guides/write-page-layout/).
