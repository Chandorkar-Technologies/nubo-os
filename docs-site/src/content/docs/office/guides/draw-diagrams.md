**Applies to:** Nubo Draw

## Before you begin

Nubo Draw is open on a blank page.

## Steps

1. Open the **Insert** tab, choose **Shapes**, and pick a rectangle. Drag on the page to draw it.
2. Type to put text inside the shape. Colour it from the **Home** tab.
3. Draw a second shape.
4. To link them, open **Home** or **Insert** and choose a connector from **Shapes**. Drag from the edge of one shape to the other. <!-- verify label: where connectors are listed in Draw -->
5. Move one of the shapes. The connector follows.
6. Line shapes up: select several and open the **Layout** tab. **Align** lines them along an edge or a centre, and **Distribute** spaces them evenly.
7. **Arrange** changes which shape is in front.
8. Add a label anywhere with **Text Box** on the Home tab.
9. Add more pages with **New Page** in Insert or Layout.

## Verify

Moving a shape moves the arrows attached to it.

## Export

Choose **File**, then **Export** to make a PDF. See [Work with PDF files](/office/guides/work-with-pdf/).
