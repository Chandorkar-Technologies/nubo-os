**Applies to:** Desktop

Nubo OS is based on Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, so it runs Ubuntu's packages, and it uses Flatpak and Flathub for most apps you add. The desktop comes with a small set of everyday apps and shows other well-known apps as grey icons that download when you click them.

Apps come from three places:

- **Flathub**, through Flatpak. This is where most user apps come from, including Firefox, Collabora Office, VLC and Spotify on a fresh install.
- **The package archive**, through apt. System parts and a few apps such as Geary come this way.
- **Websites**, as launchers that open a page in your browser.

Snaps are not used for apps on Nubo OS. See [Install and remove apps](/desktop/apps/install-and-remove-apps/).
