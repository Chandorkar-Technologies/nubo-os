All files: JPEG q90, sRGB, metadata stripped, 16:9, never upscaled. Previews (1600 px, q80) are in previews/. All images were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons originals; licence verified against the Commons file page wikitext AND the original source page (Flickr, NPGallery, DVIDS, Poly Haven, Skitterphoto) where reachable. None of these needs a credit under its licence, but credit lines are recorded for goodwill/About-page use. No CC BY images are included.
